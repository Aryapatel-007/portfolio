How I build

The same few rules show up in every project.

Verify, don't trustModel output passes through compilers, validators and tests before anyone sees it.
Local-firstYour data stays on your machine, and the café's billing works when the WiFi doesn't.
Safe by structureApproval gates, append-only ledgers and crash-safe checkpoints are part of the design, not reminders in a doc.
Measure itBenchmarks and A/B runs instead of demos that happened to go well.
